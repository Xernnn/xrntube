            Sync

            Keep every browser you use on one blocklist.

            Sync through your server

            Every browser that should share this blocklist — the laptop, the browser on your phone — points at the same small sync service (server/xrntube-sync.py in this repository) running on your own server. Enter its address and your login once on each device; the blocklist, keywords and never-block list then stay in step, with deletions carried across too.

            Backup & data

            Save everything to a file, bring a list in, or start over.

            Back up

            One file with everything: blocked channels and videos (with their sub counts, @handles and video-only exceptions), title keywords, length and comment limits, the never-block list, and your What gets hidden switches.

            Import or restore

            Takes an XrnTube backup (any version) or an export from the original BlockTube extension. You see exactly what will come in — and what can't — before anything changes, and nothing you already have is overwritten.

            Danger zone

            Deletions sync to your other devices. Each one can be undone from Recently removed on the Blocklist page — but a backup first never hurts.
